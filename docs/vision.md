# Vision — Fogbell Harbor (working title)

## One sentence
Keep a foggy harbor with your friends: check every ship against tonight's rules, let the real ones dock, and ring the fog bell on the mimics.

## Why this game, why now
- Roblox is rewarding **28-day retention and co-play** over viral spikes (RFY changes, 2026-06-15). It is also steering discovery away from saturated "steal" and "+1" games (Q2 2026 earnings coverage).
- **Judgment co-op** is proven: Animal Hospital reached 2.44B visits in 5 months. Rule-based inspection games have a teen and adult following off-platform.
- No Roblox game currently owns the "harbor keeper / ship inspection" fantasy (search, 2026-10-17).
- New games launch to **16+ players first** (Kids and Select gate). A logic-and-teamwork game fits that audience, and Mild content lets it widen to 9–15 later.

## Pillars
1. **Look closely, decide fast.** Every ship is a 10–20 s puzzle. Skill comes from observation, not spending.
2. **Wrong is funny, not punishing.** A mistake turns into slapstick chaos the crew fixes together.
3. **Every night is new.** The rules are generated, so the puzzle never repeats.
4. **The crew is the game.** Roles emerge on their own. Pings beat chat. Solo still works.
5. **Fair and honest.** No paid luck, no paid randomness, no fake timers. Purchases are cosmetic or comfort.

## Emotional arc of a night
Calm dusk → first ship ("I can do this") → second rule ("wait, check the lights") → mimic chaos (laughter, blame, teamwork) → tide 3 rush (focus) → Harbor Log (pride, plus "one more night").

## Non-goals
- No horror jump-scares or gore. The tone is whimsical coastal mystery.
- No player-vs-player theft, combat, or trading at launch.
- No open world, vehicles to drive, or building system in the MVP.
- No anime or meme IP. No brainrot characters.
- No paid random items. No sale of soft currency.

## Success, in plain terms
- **Closed test** meets the thresholds in `launch-liveops-and-metrics.md`.
- **Launch:** passes the Kids and Select evaluation and holds D7 retention we can grow from.
- **Ongoing:** a solo-sustainable weekly update cadence.
