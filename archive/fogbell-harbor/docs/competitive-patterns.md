# Competitive patterns: what Fogbell Harbor uses

Full cards: `../research/pattern-cards/`. Synthesis and pattern library (P1–P18): `../research/competitive-patterns.md`.

## Patterns adopted
| Pattern | Source games | How Fogbell Harbor uses it |
|---|---|---|
| P11 Judgment as the core verb | Animal Hospital | Inspect the ship, compare with the rules, open the gate or ring the bell |
| P7 Run resets, meta persists | 99 Nights, Dead Rails | Upgrades bought during a night reset. Rank, Logbook and cosmetics persist |
| P14 Procedural variation | Fisch, Steal An Egg mutations | Ships are built from part combinations, and rules are generated from a grammar |
| P4 Diegetic UI | Animal Hospital, RIVALS pads | Rules board, gate lamp, cargo counter and Harbor Log clipboard all live in the world |
| P1 Zero-wait join | Huss Valley, JJS | A joining player spawns on the pier mid-night with a dockhand job ready |
| P2 Failure gives you something to do | Huss Valley, MM2 spectate | A wrong call spawns the mimic chase. Being bounced into the sea means swimming back as a "spotter in the water" who can ping |
| P12 Physics payoff | Stone Skipping, Steal a Brainrot flings | Mimic rampage: crates and players bounce, and the crew nets the blob |
| P8 Shared timed conditions | Grow a Garden weather | Weekly night conditions (storm, aurora, full moon) on a truthful schedule |
| P17 Cosmetic-only monetization | JJS, DTI, Brookhaven | Outfits, lanterns, horns, dock decorations, emotes, ping skins |
| P3 One-line promise and controls | 99 Nights, JJS, Huss Valley | "Is that ship real?" Controls: look, inspect, pull or ring, carry |
| End-of-run report | Animal Hospital | The Harbor Log scores the crew and shows what is next |

## Patterns rejected
Paid luck, paid random items, player-vs-player theft, soft-currency packs, 2× money passes, admin-command passes, countdown icons for untrue events, and anime or meme IP. Reasons are in `../research/competitive-patterns.md`.

## Direct neighbours to stay distinct from
- **Animal Hospital (Anomaly):** we avoid hospitals and animal patients, sanity meters, horror, and the "Anomaly" tag in our title. Our identity: sea, ships, rules boards, slapstick.
- **Off-platform lighthouse games** (Static Dread, and other lighthouse and ship-identification titles): we avoid their names, horror radio framing, art and UI. Ours is co-op, whimsical, and built on generated rules.
