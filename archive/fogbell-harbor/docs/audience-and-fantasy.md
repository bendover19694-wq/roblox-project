# Audience and fantasy

## Segments (in launch order)
| Segment | Why they come | What keeps them | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **16–24 puzzle and co-op players** (launch-gated audience) | Recognize the "inspect and decide" format from YouTube and Steam anomaly or inspection games. Play with friends. | Mastery of tells, logbook completion, harbor upgrades | The first 250–500 highly engaged players needed for Kids and Select eligibility come from here |
| **13–15 friend groups** (after Select eligibility) | Clips of mimic chaos, playing with friends | Rank, cosmetics, weekly conditions | Pings instead of chat suit the age-based chat restrictions |
| **9–12 casual players** (after Select eligibility) | Cute ships, funny blob, simple icon rules | The first-night rules use 1 icon. Short nights | Readability tested with icons, not words |
| **Solo players** | A quiet puzzle at their own pace | Logbook, rank | The ship rate scales down when playing alone |

## Age suitability
- Target content maturity: **Mild**. Fallback: Minimal, if the mimic is fully cartoonish.
- Questionnaire answers to plan for: no violence beyond cartoon slapstick (players get bounced into the water and respawn on the pier), no blood, no gambling, no paid random items, and no romance, crude humour, or real-world references.
- Mimics are goofy rather than scary: a jelly blob with googly eyes that mimics ship parts badly.

## Fantasy
"I'm a harbor keeper on a weird foggy coast where the sea makes copies of things." The player is the sharp-eyed expert their crew relies on. The pleasure is **noticing**: the wrong flag, a lantern blinking out of rhythm, a hull with no reflection.

## Promise on the game page
- **Icon:** one ship silhouette half out of the fog, with a single wrong detail (an eye on the sail), and a keeper holding a spyglass. No text, or at most one word.
- **Title:** see the name candidates in `launch-liveops-and-metrics.md`.
- **Description, first line:** "Is that ship real? Check it, dock it, or ring the bell!"

## Player motivations served
| Motivation | Where it is served |
|---|---|
| Competence | Fast, correct calls; rank titles |
| Social belonging | Crew roles, pings, shared chaos |
| Curiosity and collection | Logbook of ship variants and mimic tells |
| Expression | Keeper outfit, lantern colour, horn sound, dock decorations |
| Novelty | Generated rules every night, weekly conditions |
