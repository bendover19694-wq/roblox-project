# Video research: design constraints carried forward

Each constraint cites its basis. These feed the concept scorecard and, later, the design bible.

| # | Constraint | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Optimize for **28-day organic retention**, not a launch spike. Trend sims that die in about a month score badly. | Documented (RFY D8–28 window); Tizzy post-mortem |
| 2 | **First 3 minutes are measured.** The first meaningful action happens within 15 s or less. No menu, loading, or tutorial wall. Teach secondary systems through contextual tutorials. | Documented (bounce <60 s / 61–180 s); both creators |
| 3 | The icon, title, and first action promise the **same fantasy**. | Tizzy (first-step drop from a mismatched title); PTR Documented |
| 4 | **Co-play is a ranking signal.** Friends must make the game better, not just be allowed in. | Documented (intentional co-play days) |
| 5 | Sessions of **20–40 min** with a clear end point and a return reason. Playtime past 60 min/day earns no ranking credit. | Documented (60 min cap) |
| 6 | Monetize through **spend days**, not big one-offs: cheap, repeatable, transparent, not pay-to-win. **No soft-currency packs.** Paid randomness and limited stock only after a policy check. | Documented (spend days, Robux per user); Tizzy regrets |
| 7 | **One verb a 9-year-old gets in 5 s, plus emergent depth** for 13+ players. | Tizzy; age data (Unverified) |
| 8 | **Clippable moments** and **visible flex** are designed, not hoped for. | Tizzy (Inferred mechanism) |
| 9 | **Mobile and console parity at slice level.** Fresh-account playtests. A published private test place before launch. | Tizzy |
| 10 | **Telemetry from day one:** an FTUE step funnel, progression milestone funnels, and a shop funnel. | Tizzy |
| 11 | **Art style guide before any asset.** The style must be achievable by one person. | Tizzy |
| 12 | **No paid ads** until organic PTR, bounce, and D1 pass closed-test thresholds. Ad-acquired users don't count toward ranking. | Documented |
| 13 | Revenue planning uses **cohort ARPDAU × realistic DAU with decay**, never weekend run-rate. | Tizzy (both videos); Inferred |

## Open tension to test, not argue
- Intro cutscene vs. straight into play: rasdev says no intro; Tizzy reports a bounce reduction from a cutscene. Plan: a skippable in-world intro of 8 s or less vs. none, A/B measured on both bounce segments.
