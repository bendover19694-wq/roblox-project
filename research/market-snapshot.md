## 6. Implications for our concept work
1. **Do not enter steal/base-raid or "+1" incrementals.** They are at peak saturation, sit on a proven 90–99% decay curve, and Roblox is publicly de-emphasizing them. One of them is the planned "tempting but saturated" rejected concept.
2. **Target the durable profile:** session-based co-op or skill play with social rules, plus a 28-day progression or collection meta layer. Evidence: 99 Nights, MM2, RIVALS, DTI, and Fisch.
3. **Watch-list themes** (rising, not yet saturated): "anomaly"-style shift work, skill-based sports and movement arenas, and creative-competition formats outside fashion. Any of these can be used *with an original wedge*, never as a reskin.
4. **Monetization model to emulate:** cosmetic/QoL passes (the JJS and DTI pattern), plus transparent repeatable developer products. Avoid the 2× money and admin-command pattern.
5. **Server size:** viral steal games run 7–8-player servers, while social and roleplay games run 25–40. Pick server size to fit the core interaction. It is a design lever, not a default.
