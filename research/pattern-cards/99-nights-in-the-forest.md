# 99 Nights in the Forest — pattern card
**Category:** co-op survival / tension. **A durable top-5 game.**

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):** 475,973 playing · 30.1B visits · server size 25 · created 2025-03-04 · 90% like · 1 pass (Decorator, 199)

**Evidence:**
- Observed: description ("Build a camp with friends. Something is watching you.") and the pass.
- Unverified: guides on Diamonds and classes.
- Monster and rescue details from prior knowledge (Inferred).

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: friend groups aged about 9–16 who like mild horror and co-op.
- Fantasy: surviving a haunted forest together.
- Promise: a single line that names both **co-op and threat**.

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** in the forest with friends next to a campfire. Immediate unease.
- **60 s:** gather wood and feed the fire.
- **5 min:** first night. The threat appears, and the group scrambles back to the light.
- **30 min:** the camp is upgraded, the fire's radius has grown and opened more map, first rescue objective or chest, first death and recovery.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** gather → fuel and craft → survive the night.
- **Meta:** classes bought with Diamonds, upgraded across runs.
- **Social:** role division, saving friends.
- **Event:** seasonal updates.
- **Return tomorrow:** class progress, daily quests, "can we reach night N."

## 4. Economy
- In-run: resources, reset each run.
- Persistent: Diamonds. Free sources are strongholds, daily quests, badges, and milestones, at about 12–15/h for a skilled team (Unverified).
- Classes cost about 10–200 Diamonds.
- Diamonds are also sold for Robux.
- **The run-reset plus persistent-meta split** limits inflation.

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- Minimal HUD: health/hunger, a small inventory, a night counter.
- The fire's light radius is the **diegetic safety indicator**.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Darkness and sound do most of the work: anticipation, sudden reveals, the fire as relief.
- Low-cost horror, made of lighting and audio more than VFX.

## 7. Social
- Teamwork, rescue moments, and highly clippable scares.
- 25-player servers, with friend parties inside them.

## 8. Monetization
- A single cosmetic pass (Observed).
- Diamonds for Robux are a shortcut to classes, which is a convenience/power gray zone.
- The first offer probably comes at the class selection screen.

## 9. Live-ops
- Regular content and seasonal events, plus a strong creator scene (scares make good clips).

## 10. Friction and risks
- Night difficulty walls.
- Griefing in public servers.
- Horror intensity versus the youngest players (content maturity label).
- Paid Diamonds shorten class grinds.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable:**
  - **run-based co-op with persistent class meta**
  - a diegetic safe radius that grows as you invest
  - lighting and audio as cheap tension
  - a one-line promise naming co-op plus threat
- **Protected:** the forest and night premise, its monsters, the "99 Nights" naming, its map.
- **Our alternative:** a different shared-threat fantasy with an original threat. Run-based co-op where a **group-invested safe zone** expands, and persistent roles or classes are earned through play.
