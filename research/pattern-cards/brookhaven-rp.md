# Brookhaven RP — pattern card
**Category:** social roleplay / expression (evergreen)

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):** 672,120 playing · 88.6B visits · server size 30 · created 2020-04-21 · 86% like · 18 passes (35–749): houses, vehicles, music, pets, estates, VIP. **No combat power is sold.**

**Evidence:** Observed: the description, including private-server benefits. Loop details are Inferred.

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: a broad young audience who want to hang out and pretend.
- Fantasy: living a life in a small town.
- Promise: "Be whoever you want to be."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** spawn in town. Houses, cars, and other players are visible at once.
- **60 s:** claim a free house and pick an avatar outfit.
- **5 min:** drive a car and join or start a roleplay scene.
- **30 min:** a regular friend group, house customization, and the paid properties you'd like.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** emergent roleplay, player-made stories.
- **Meta:** properties, vehicles, props.
- **Social:** this *is* the game.
- **Event:** regular new props and estates.
- **Return tomorrow:** friends, and private servers that keep their props.

## 4. Economy
- Almost no grind economy. Value comes from access to expression assets.

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- A minimal side menu (avatar, house, vehicle, tools). The world is the interface.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Low intensity. Vehicles, doors, and props. Players supply the emotional content.

## 7. Social
- The biggest strength and the biggest safety risk.
- Roleplay with strangers needs strong moderation and private-server tools (props locking, owner checks; Observed).

## 8. Monetization
- Expression and access passes, plus private-server perks (Observed).
- Priced low to mid.

## 9. Live-ops
- Small, frequent prop and estate drops.

## 10. Friction and risks
- Moderation load, inappropriate roleplay, and network-effect incumbency. Head-to-head entry is very hard.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable:**
  - **expression passes instead of power passes**
  - private-server utility perks
  - free housing as an anchor
- **Protected:** the town identity, map, and branding.
- **Our alternative:** add a light *expression layer* (a customizable base, outfit, or vehicle) to our core loop, with private-server perks. Do not compete in open roleplay.
