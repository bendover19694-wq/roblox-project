# Dress To Impress — pattern card
**Category:** expression and identity, plus short-round competition

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):** 351,004 playing · 11.2B visits · server size 13 · created 2023-10-18 · 91% like · 14 passes (129–799), mostly **outfit sets**, plus Run Faster, an Item Limit pass, ×2 Money, and VIP

**Evidence:** official description (Observed). Round details from prior knowledge (Inferred).

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: a broad young audience that leans female. It strongly reaches players who are underserved elsewhere.
- Fantasy: being the top model.
- Promise: "Create outfits for a theme, walk the runway, get voted."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** the lobby announces a round theme and a timer.
- **60 s:** you're in the dressing room picking items under time pressure.
- **5 min:** the runway walk with poses, then star voting, a placement, and a reward.
- **30 min:** several rounds, a level-up, and your first wishlist of paid sets.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** theme → create under a timer → present → peer vote.
- **Meta:** levels, item unlocks, money.
- **Social:** voting, collaborating, competing.
- **Event:** seasonal themes and lore events.
- **Return tomorrow:** new items and themes, and the pull of ranking.

## 4. Economy
- Money from placement buys items.
- Sets sold as passes.
- The scarcity is in **creative options**, not power.

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- A dense dressing-room inventory in a grid.
- A theme banner and timer are always visible.
- A runway screen and a voting overlay.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Runway poses, a camera that follows the model, a reveal of placements.
- Most of the emotional work is done by **being seen and judged**.

## 7. Social
- Peer judgment is the game.
- Risks: troll voting and bias against players in default avatars (Inferred).

## 8. Monetization
- Cosmetic outfit sets and VIP (Observed). Ethical and high-value.

## 9. Live-ops
- New item drops and story or lore events.

## 10. Friction and risks
- Voting fairness, timer stress for younger players, and a crowded copycat field (Facecard: Makeup Showdown is rising).

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable:**
  - **create under constraint → present → peer vote**
  - a theme as a fresh prompt every round
  - cosmetic sets as the monetization
- **Protected:** the fashion runway identity, item art, and naming.
- **Our alternative:** apply "create under a theme and timer, then show and vote" to a *different creative object*, such as builds, contraptions, or performances. Mitigate troll votes with weighted or skill-calibrated voting.
