# Steal a Brainrot — pattern card
**Category:** high social chaos / steal / trading

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):** 150,475 playing · 74.0B visits · server size 8 · created 2025-05-16 · 85% like · 3 passes (2× Money 299, VIP 375, Admin Commands 7,999)

**Evidence:**
- Observed: description and passes.
- Unverified: press reports of a 25.4–25.8M CCU peak in October 2025, lawsuits against imitators (Bloomberg, 2026-03-24), and Lucky Block guides.
- Loop details are Inferred.

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: young players who love chaos and trolling.
- Fantasy: owning and stealing absurd meme characters.
- Promise: "Buy brainrots, steal others', get rich, troll."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** spawn at your base. Characters roll past on a conveyor.
- **60 s:** buy one. It walks to your base and starts generating cash per second.
- **5 min:** see someone carrying a character out of your base, or steal one yourself. First base lock.
- **30 min:** rebirth decision, slap or troll gear, chasing rarer characters.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** buy or steal → place → passive income → buy rarer.
- **Meta:** rebirths and rarity tiers.
- **Social:** theft, defense, trolling.
- **Event:** admin-abuse events with exclusive drops.
- **Return tomorrow:** your collection persists, plus FOMO about rare drops.

## 4. Economy
- Cash per second from owned units.
- Scarcity: conveyor RNG plus event-only units.
- Base lock is a timed defense.
- Sinks: purchases, rebirth resets.

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- Minimal HUD: cash and a few buttons.
- Most information is **diegetic**: the conveyor, your base, players carrying things.
- The shop holds Robux items, including Lucky Blocks.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Comedic character animations and sounds.
- Theft is a visible carry back across the map, which is **clippable**.
- Slap, knockback, and fling physics.

## 7. Social
- Theft causes real distress for some children (press).
- Constant trolling.
- Trading and scams.
- Server size 8 keeps encounters frequent.

## 8. Monetization
- **Lucky Blocks are paid random loot**, buyable with Robux (Unverified guides; about 2,399 R for the top tier).
- 2× money pass.
- **A 7,999 R admin-commands pass.**
- High monetization pressure on children.

## 9. Live-ops
- Frequent admin-abuse events and themed units. These spike CCU.

## 10. Friction and risks
- About 99% decline from peak.
- Gambling-adjacent design and child-wellbeing criticism (opinion press).
- Massive clone saturation and IP lawsuits.
- Weak skill expression.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable:**
  - visible, risky carry-back as a tension moment
  - diegetic information (the world shows the state)
  - small servers for frequent encounters
- **Protected:** the brainrot characters and meme IP, the "Steal a ___" identity, the conveyor-shop layout.
- **Reject:** this genre (saturated, decaying, ethically fraught). Use it as the "tempting but saturated" example.
- **Our alternative:** consensual risk, such as an opt-in contested zone. Players come back with something earned, and loss is partial and recoverable, never your whole collection.
