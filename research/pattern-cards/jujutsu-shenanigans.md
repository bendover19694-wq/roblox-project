# Jujutsu Shenanigans — pattern card
**Category:** mastery / arena fighting (battlegrounds)

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):** 185,589 playing · 7.65B visits · server size 20 · created 2022-04-17 · 86% like · 5 passes (115–345): Awakening Outfits, Custom Kill Sound, More Build Saves, More Emote Slots, Early Access. **Every pass is cosmetic, QoL, or timing. None sells power.**

**Evidence:** Observed: the description lists the full control scheme. Everything else is Inferred.

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: action fans aged about 11–18 who enjoy combo mastery.
- Fantasy: an anime-style sorcerer brawler.
- Promise: the controls fit on one line (M1 combo, 1–4 skills, Q dash, F block, R special, G awaken).

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** pick a character and you're already in the free-for-all arena.
- **60 s:** you land your first combo by mashing M1, and die quickly to someone better.
- **5 min:** you learn block and dash timing, and reach your first awakening.
- **30 min:** you have a main character, can read opponents, and use emotes to taunt.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** engage, combo, escape, finish. Fights take seconds.
- **Meta:** a character roster, build mode, cosmetics.
- **Social:** rivalries, taunts, private servers.
- **Event:** character drops with early access.
- **Return tomorrow:** skill improvement, plus new characters to try.

## 4. Economy
- Thin. There is little persistent currency pressure, and almost all value comes from skill.

## 5. UI map
- A small ability bar, health, and an awakening meter. All screen space goes to combat.
- A clear PC key scheme. Mobile and console are adapted (Inferred).

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Destruction physics, hit-stop, knockback, camera shake.
- Most of the emotional work is done by **impact feel**.

## 7. Social
- Clippable highlights and montage culture.
- Toxicity and teaming are risks in free-for-all.

## 8. Monetization
- Cosmetic and QoL only (Observed). A strong example of "fun first, pay for flair."

## 9. Live-ops
- Character updates, with an early-access pass for timing.

## 10. Friction and risks
- Anime IP adjacency (inspired by an existing anime).
- The skill gap is brutal for new players.
- The genre is saturated: TSB, Legends Battlegrounds, many clones.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable:**
  - **a one-line control grammar**
  - impact feel as the product
  - cosmetic-and-QoL-only monetization
  - early access as an ethical paid perk
- **Protected:** the characters, moves, and names, which echo anime IP. Avoid that adjacency.
- **Our alternative:** if our concept has action, use a tiny readable verb set with deep timing. Sell flair only.
