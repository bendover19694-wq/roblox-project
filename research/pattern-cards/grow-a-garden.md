# Grow a Garden — pattern card
**Category:** idle / collection / economy

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):** 25,484 playing · 36.0B visits · server size 4 · created 2025-03-25 · 90% like · 0 game passes

**Evidence:** the official description (Observed), the RFY lifecycle (§3 of the snapshot), and guides plus prior knowledge (Inferred). Not played by us.

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: young casual players and collectors.
- Fantasy: tending your own garden that grows while you're away.
- Promise: "Plant, wait, harvest, profit — and flex your rare fruit."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** spawn on your own plot. Seeds are already in the hotbar.
- **60 s:** equip a seed and click a brown plot. It sprouts visibly.
- **5 min:** harvest, sell at the in-world stand, and buy seeds from a shop whose stock **restocks on a timer**.
- **30 min:** bigger and rarer crops, gear, eggs and pets. First weather event and mutation.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** plant → wait → harvest → sell → buy seeds.
- **Meta:** rarer seeds, mutations (size or variant multipliers), pets that boost growth.
- **Social:** visit or flex gardens, trading.
- **Event:** weather and global events that mutate crops, plus weekly updates.
- **Return tomorrow:** crops keep growing offline, and the restock timer rewards checking back.

## 4. Economy
- One soft currency.
- Scarcity control: **limited shop stock per restock** (time-gated, the same for everyone).
- Sinks: seeds, gear, eggs.
- Value multipliers: mutations.
- Inflation risk: high once trading and admin-event giveaways stack up.

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- Persistent: currency, a hotbar of seeds and tools, a few top buttons.
- Shops are **physical NPC stands in the world**, not a permanent menu.
- The restock countdown is the key piece of contextual information.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Crops grow continuously, which is ambient progress you can see from anywhere.
- Global weather changes the sky and lighting for everyone, which is a shared moment.
- Emotional driver: **anticipation** (restock, weather, an unknown mutation outcome).

## 7. Social
- Tiny servers of 4 (Observed) keep the plot area manageable.
- Status comes from fruit size and rarity.
- Trading invites scams.
- The sequel GAG2 (2026-06-12) added **night-time crop stealing** (Unverified press), moving toward the steal meta.

## 8. Monetization
- No passes (Observed). All revenue is assumed to come from **developer products**: instant restock or rare-seed buys, skips, eggs (Inferred).
- First paid offer is likely at the first sold-out restock.

## 9. Live-ops
- Weekly updates and "admin abuse" style live events drive concurrency spikes (Unverified).

## 10. Friction and risks
- About 85–99% decline from its 2025 peak. Named by Roblox as a lost-momentum example (Documented press).
- Economy blowouts and trading scams.
- Offline-growth loops get repetitive without depth.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable:**
  - offline progress plus a shared timed restock as a return hook
  - global world events that change everyone's outcomes
  - shops placed in the world
  - small servers for plot games
- **Protected:** the garden and seed theme, crop art, names, UI look.
- **Our alternative:** a different fantasy where a *shared world condition* (for example a tide, season, or storm) rotates on a visible timer and changes what every player's creations do. Combine it with **skill input** so outcomes are not only waiting.
