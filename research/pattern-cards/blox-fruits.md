# Blox Fruits — pattern card
**Category:** long-term mastery / combat / RPG

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):**
- 268,924 playing · 65.0B visits · server size 12 · created 2019-01-16 · 92% like
- Level cap 3000 (from the game description)
- 6 passes, priced 350–2,700 Robux:
  - Fast Boats
  - 2× Boss Drops
  - 2× Money
  - 2× Mastery
  - Dark Blade
  - Fruit Notifier at 2,700

**Evidence:** official description and passes (Observed). Loop details from prior knowledge (Inferred).

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- **Audience:** players aged about 10–17 who like anime-style action and long grinds.
- **Fantasy:** going from nobody to the strongest fighter on the seas.
- **Promise:** "Master the sword or a fruit power."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** pick a faction, take a starter quest, and fight weak NPCs.
- **60 s:** first level-ups, with stat points to assign.
- **5 min:** first boss or island. You learn that powerful fruits exist.
- **30 min:** about level 30–60, plus a boat, a new island, and a first fruit (a spawn or a roll).

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** quest → kill NPCs → level → stats.
- **Meta:** fruits, swords, fighting styles, mastery, seas, raids.
- **Social:** PvP bounty, crews, fruit trading.
- **Event:** updates, many years of them.
- **Return tomorrow:** fruit stock rotation, the grind toward the next sea, raids.

## 4. Economy
- Money and fragments.
- Fruit rarity sets value, with a large trading economy.
- Fruits can be bought for Robux (Inferred).
- Fruit Notifier pass at 2,700.

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- A busy HUD: level, health and energy bars, an ability bar, a quest panel.
- Desktop-centric, with mobile adapted.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Showy ability VFX, combo hits, screen shake.
- Every fruit's moveset works as a showcase.

## 7. Social
- Bounty hunting, trading scams, flexing rare fruits.
- Huge YouTube ecosystem.

## 8. Monetization
- Multiplier and power passes (Observed).
- Paid fruits (Inferred).
- A pay-to-win perception is present.

## 9. Live-ops
- Large, infrequent updates that are hyped for a long time.
- Has lasted 7+ years.

## 10. Friction and risks
- Very long grind with a steep complexity curve for new players.
- Pay-to-win.
- Needs a large content team.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable patterns:**
  - **mastery per build** (each weapon or power has its own mastery)
  - a long arc of zones as visible next goals
- **Protected:** the fruit and pirate-anime identity (close to existing anime IP), its movesets.
- **For a solo creator:** content scope makes this genre unrealistic. Borrow only "mastery per loadout."
