# Murder Mystery 2 — pattern card
**Category:** short-round social deduction (evergreen)

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):** 309,112 playing · 31.4B visits · server size 12 · created **2014-01-18** · 91% like · 3 passes: Radio 475, Elite 499, a cosmetic bundle 3,399

**Evidence:** Observed: the description gives the three roles. Everything else is Inferred.

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: a broad audience aged about 9–16.
- Fantasy: suspense with a secret identity.
- Promise: three roles explained in three sentences (Innocent, Sheriff, Murderer).

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** a lobby, then a short countdown.
- **60 s:** you're on the map with a secret role, collecting coins and watching other players.
- **5 min:** two or three rounds done, you've died at least once, and you're spectating.
- **30 min:** you've played all the roles, seen the knife and gun cosmetics, and met trading.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** a round of about 2–3 minutes with a hidden role and asymmetric goals.
- **Meta:** cosmetic knives and guns, levels.
- **Social:** suspicion, accusation, and a **trading economy**.
- **Event:** seasonal cosmetics.
- **Return tomorrow:** the round variety, plus cosmetics and trading.

## 4. Economy
- Coins collected during rounds.
- Cosmetic weapons have rarity, with community-made value lists.
- The trading economy is huge.

## 5. UI map
- Very little UI: a role reveal card, a coin counter, then the world.
- A clear spectate state after death.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- The role reveal moment, the knife throw, the sheriff's shot.
- Tension comes from uncertainty, not from VFX.

## 7. Social
- Built-in drama ("it's them!") and clips.
- Trading scams are a long-standing risk.

## 8. Monetization
- Few passes. Most revenue comes from cosmetic crates and bundles (Inferred).
- Paid random crates need a policy check.

## 9. Live-ops
- Seasonal cosmetic events across 12 years.

## 10. Friction and risks
- Scam and value-list culture, round-length downtime while dead, griefing.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable:**
  - **hidden asymmetric roles in short rounds**
  - the role reveal as a ritual
  - spectating as engagement after death
  - cosmetic identity on your tool
- **Protected:** the murder/sheriff framing and the MM2 identity and map style.
- **Our alternative:** if we use rounds, make them **short with a role twist** in an original non-violent fantasy. Death turns into a meaningful spectator or helper role, not dead time.
