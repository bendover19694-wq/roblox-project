# Fisch — pattern card
**Category:** collection / mastery (durable)

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):**
- 358,975 playing · 5.03B visits · server size 20 · created 2024-03-13 · 91% like
- 9 passes (99–499 Robux): Sell, Appraise, Enchant, and Spawn Boat "Anywhere", Appraiser's Luck, Double XP, emote and bobber packs
- The description claims "400,000+ possible variations" of fish

**Evidence:**
- Observed: the description and passes.
- Inferred: everything else.

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: relaxed collectors aged about 10–18.
- Fantasy: an explorer-angler chasing legendary fish.
- Promise: "Cast your line to catch rare fish; explore."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** you have a starter rod. Hold to cast.
- **60 s:** you get a bite, play the shake/reel minigame, and catch your first fish.
- **5 min:** you sell fish, upgrade bait or your rod, and see the bestiary fill.
- **30 min:** you sail to a new island, appraise a fish, and see your first mutation or rare catch.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** cast → bite → reel a skill minigame → catch.
- **Meta:** rods, islands, bestiary completion, enchants, appraisal.
- **Social:** fishing side by side and showing off rare catches.
- **Event:** limited fish and events.
- **Return tomorrow:** the bestiary gaps and rotating conditions.

## 4. Economy
- Money from selling fish.
- Fish variation (size, mutation) creates value.
- Sinks: rods and enchants.
- **Convenience passes** remove travel, but do not add power (Observed).

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- A minimal fishing HUD.
- The reel minigame is a small, focused overlay.
- The bestiary and inventory are opened on demand.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Line tension, the splash, the catch reveal with a size/mutation tag.
- Most of the emotional work is done by **the "what did I catch" reveal combined with skill timing**.

## 7. Social
- Low-pressure co-presence, which is good for younger players and safety.

## 8. Monetization
- "Anywhere" convenience, luck, and XP passes.
- A luck pass sits in a mild gray zone.

## 9. Live-ops
- Regular islands and limited fish.

## 10. Friction and risks
- Grind at the high end, and repetition.
- Copycats such as "Fish It!" (#22) exist.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable patterns:**
  - **a skill minigame inside a collection loop**
  - a bestiary as the long goal
  - convenience passes that remove travel rather than add power
  - variation (size, mutation) as cheap content multiplication
- **Protected:** the fishing identity, fish art, and naming.
- **Our alternative:** a collection loop where each acquisition takes a short skill input, with procedural variation used to multiply a small art set.
