# +1 Stone Skipping — pattern card
**Category:** idle / incremental. **Rising (new in 2026), part of the "+1" trend.**

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):**
- 135,461 playing · 50.6M visits · server size 10
- Created **2026-09-05** · **97% like** (the highest in our set)
- 14 passes (25–599 Robux): Auto Wins 25, Hatch +3 Eggs 25, +Pet slots, training zones, aura slots, Auto Rebirth, and others

**Evidence:**
- Observed: the description and passes.
- Unverified (guides from games.gg and allthings.how): worlds and territories.

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: young, casual players who enjoy number-go-up games.
- Fantasy: an impossibly long stone skip.
- Promise: "Every bounce gives you +1 Skill."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** you throw a stone. It bounces, and +1 numbers pop up.
- **60 s:** your second throw goes further and you see your first Wins.
- **5 min:** you're on a training pad, buy a better stone, and hatch your first pet.
- **30 min:** you pass your first portal skill check and reach a new territory, with a rebirth on the horizon.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** throw → bounces → Skill → longer throw → Wins.
- **Meta:** 5 worlds × 9 territories, rebirths, pets, relics, auras.
- **Social:** minimal (showing off distance and auras).
- **Return tomorrow:** the next territory and rebirth targets.

## 4. Economy
- Skill grows from throws and passively from training pads.
- Wins buy stones, eggs, and relics.
- Rebirth resets progress and raises your cap.

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- Big counters plus a Throw button.
- The next portal's requirement is visible as a goal.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- The stone arc and splash bounces, a +1 popup on every bounce, and the camera following the throw.
- Most of the emotional work is done by **watching the throw fly**: one action, a long payoff.

## 7. Social
- Weak. Players are mostly solo in the same server.

## 8. Monetization
- **Very cheap stacking entry passes (25 R)**, then auto-play, capacity, and zone upgrades (Observed).
- A heavy auto/idle bias.

## 9. Live-ops
- A sequel or variant listing ("Stone Skipping +1 - NEXT") exists already.

## 10. Friction and risks
- A trend template with little depth beyond numbers.
- Templated clones.
- Decay is likely to follow the "+1" fad.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable patterns:**
  - **one input followed by a long, watchable physics payoff** (great for clips)
  - an absurd item list ("even a DONUT")
  - a visible next-zone requirement
  - a 25 R entry price point
- **Protected:** the "+1 ___" title format, which is template-saturated.
- **Our alternative:** use a physics payoff moment as the *reward beat* of a deeper loop, not as the whole game.
