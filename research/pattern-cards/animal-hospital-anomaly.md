# Animal Hospital (Anomaly) — pattern card
**Category:** co-op tension / job-shift horror. **Rising theme.**

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):**
- 55,128 playing · 2.44B visits in about 5 months · server size 30 · created **2026-05-10** · 93% like · 0 passes

**Unverified (tracker):** peak about 737K (per Rolimon's, via a guide).

**Evidence:**
- Observed: description ("hired to work the night shift… some patients are acting weird").
- Unverified: guides (allthings.how, GosuGamers).

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- **Audience:** friends aged about 10–16 who like spot-the-difference and mild horror.
- **Fantasy:** night-shift vet at a hospital where some patients aren't animals.
- **Promise:** "Treat patients. Notice what's wrong."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** you clock in. A patient arrives at the front desk.
- **60 s:** you inspect the animal and decide to admit it or reject it.
- **5 min:** you treat the animal using the computers and tests. The first anomaly gets through and your sanity drops.
- **30 min:** later shifts escalate with more patients, subtler anomalies, and more pressure.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** inspect → judge (real or anomaly) → act (treat or reject).
- **Meta:** shifts progress and difficulty escalates.
- **Social:** the team splits up the desk work and backs each other up.
- **Return tomorrow:** reaching the next shift.

## 4. Economy
- **Sanity** is the main pressure resource.
- No passes (Observed). Revenue model unknown.

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- A sanity meter.
- In-world computers and tests act as the interface (**diegetic UI**).

## 6. Motion and feedback
- A slow build of unease, with subtle tells to spot on each patient.
- Getting a judgment wrong produces a scary consequence.

## 7. Social
- Players argue over each call ("is that one real?").
- That shared judging is the **social story** worth telling.

## 8. Monetization
- No passes found. Products not visible.

## 9. Live-ops
- Shift content keeps evolving.
- Copycats and the "anomaly" tag are spreading: Sell Fries! [Anomaly] and the derivative Raise an Anomaly.

## 10. Friction and risks
- Spot-the-difference content gets used up, so the game needs a steady supply of new anomalies.
- The theme is starting to become a trend tag.
- The server size is unclear: 30 per the API vs. 4 per one guide.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable patterns:**
  - **judgment as the core verb** (inspect → decide → consequence)
  - diegetic interface tools
  - a shift structure with escalating difficulty
  - group debate as built-in social content
- **Protected:** the animal hospital setting, its anomaly designs, and the name.
- **Our alternative:** a judgment-based co-op job in an original setting. Each **"tell" is generated from rules (procedural combinations)**, so content doesn't run out. The game should reward observation skill rather than leaning on jump scares.
