# RIVALS — pattern card
**Category:** short-round competition / FPS

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):**
- 398,278 playing · 18.8B visits · server size 40 · created 2024-05-26 · 94% like
- Favorites: 95.0M (the highest in our set)
- 8 passes, 59–1,425 Robux (weapon/skin bundles)

**Evidence:**
- Observed: the description ("1v1 to 5v5… first to 5 wins… step on a duel pad… keys… contracts… win streak").
- Inferred: everything else.

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: competitive players aged about 11–18.
- Fantasy: proving you're the better shot.
- Promise: "Duel anyone; first to 5 wins."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** you're in a lobby full of players and duel pads.
- **60 s:** you step on a pad, get matched instantly, and are in a duel.
- **5 min:** two or three duels done, your first key, your first weapon unlock.
- **30 min:** a preferred loadout, your first contract finished, a streak attempt.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** a duel lasting minutes, first to 5.
- **Meta:** keys → weapons and skins, contracts, ranked play.
- **Social:** challenge friends on pads, spectate in the lobby, streak flexing.
- **Event:** seasonal updates such as "RIVALSWEEN".
- **Return tomorrow:** contracts, plus wanting to rank up.

## 4. Economy
- Keys are earned by playing.
- Weapon unlocks give some sidegrade value. Cosmetic wraps and skins carry most of the value.
- Bundles are sold.

## 5. UI map
- The lobby is the menu: duel pads are a **physical matchmaking UI**.
- A minimal HUD in matches.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Crisp hit feedback, an elimination reveal, score ticks.
- Skins show off flex in every duel.

## 7. Social
- Spectators gather around active pads in the lobby, which gives a social proof loop.
- 40-player servers.

## 8. Monetization
- Weapon and skin bundles (Observed). Cases are possible (Inferred; needs a policy check).

## 9. Live-ops
- A numbered update cadence (Update 24) with seasonal themes.

## 10. Friction and risks
- Skill wall for new players, smurfing, and toxicity.
- FPS on mobile and controller needs aim assist.
- High production polish is required.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable patterns:**
  - **physical matchmaking pads in a social lobby**, which double as spectating
  - first-to-N short matches
  - contracts as daily goals
  - win streak as visible status
- **Protected:** the RIVALS identity, its weapons and skins, and its UI.
- **Our alternative:** use pad-based matchmaking for an original non-shooter skill contest. The lobby itself becomes the stage.
