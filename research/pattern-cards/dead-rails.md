# Dead Rails — pattern card
**Category:** co-op survival / expedition

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):**
- 24,028 playing · 6.61B visits · server size 16 · created 2025-01-01 · 93% like
- 4 passes: +5 Storage 95, three starter weapons at 148–175

**Trend (Unverified tracker):** 79K peak on 2025-11-28, down 38% over 30 days.

**Evidence:**
- Observed: description and passes.
- Unverified: the tracker figures.
- Loop details from prior knowledge (Inferred).

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- **Audience:** friend groups who enjoy shooters and survival.
- **Fantasy:** a train journey through a zombie-filled Wild West in 1899.
- **Promise:** "Board your train and fight through to the cure."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** you start on a train with friends. The throttle and the coal furnace are visible.
- **60 s:** the train starts moving and the track unrolls into the distance.
- **5 min:** first stop at a town. You loot it while zombies close in and the fuel runs low.
- **30 min:** night falls and the horde arrives. You either reach the end or the run dies.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** drive → stop → scavenge → defend → fuel up → drive.
- **Meta:** you unlock classes using a persistent currency (Inferred).
- **Social:** the crew splits roles (driver, looter, gunner).
- **Event:** themed updates (the name tags show [HEATWAVE] and [ENDLESS]).
- **Return tomorrow:** class unlocks and the "reach the end" goal.

## 4. Economy
- Everything you loot during a run resets when it ends. The persistent unlock currency carries over.
- Fuel is the pacing sink inside a run.
- Starter weapons are sold as passes (Observed), which gives a mild power edge.

## 5. UI map
- Distance-to-goal is the main progress indicator.
- Fuel and health are shown on the train itself (Inferred).

## 6. Motion and feedback
- The constant motion of the train creates momentum.
- Gunfire impact feedback.
- Night as a deadline.

## 7. Social
- Co-op roles and rescue moments.
- The train makes a natural shared base you all ride in.

## 8. Monetization
- Cheap starter weapons and a storage pass (Observed).

## 9. Live-ops
- Mode variants (ENDLESS) and seasonal themes.

## 10. Friction and risks
- The content runs out (one fixed route), so CCU declines once the route is "solved."
- The shooter feel is hard on mobile (Inferred).

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable patterns:**
  - a **moving shared base** that gives a natural pace
  - the run → stop → scavenge rhythm
  - distance-to-goal as the main progress bar
- **Protected:** the 1899 zombie train premise and the Dead Rails identity.
- **Lesson:** a fixed route has a content ceiling. Procedural or rotating routes are needed for retention past day 28.
