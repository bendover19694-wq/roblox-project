# Huss Valley — pattern card
**Category:** short-round competition / movement tag. **Rising (new in 2026).**

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):**
- 105,239 playing · 141M visits in 7 weeks · server size 30
- Created **2026-08-31** · 88% like · description is just "RUN!!"
- 7 passes (99–1,799 Robux): cosmetic knives, a Premium Pass and a Halloween Premium Pass (799 each), VIP, ×2 EXP

**Evidence:**
- Observed: the API data above.
- Unverified (guides from Beebom, ProGameGuides, games.gg): the mechanics.
- Unverified (rasdev video): the first-minute claims.

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: a broad, young, action-casual crowd.
- Fantasy: escaping a chaser using slick movement.
- Promise: one word, "RUN!!", with the icon showing the action (per rasdev).

## 2. Player experience by time
- **15 s:** you join *mid-round already running*, so you never sit out (rasdev).
- **60 s:** you get caught and switch to the chaser team, which makes losing fun.
- **5 min:** you learn the momentum and side-dash movement, and make your first successful finish-line crossing.
- **30 min:** you buy your first permanent ability with Gems and use Coins for consumables (guides).

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** runners cross the arena 3 times; one knife hit converts a runner to a chaser.
- **Meta:** Gems buy permanent abilities; Coins buy match consumables.
- **Social:** an infection-style role flip.
- **Event:** a Halloween pass.
- **Return tomorrow:** movement mastery, ability unlocks, and a premium pass track.

## 4. Economy
- Two currencies earned at the end of each match based on performance, so **losses still pay**.
- Codes give extra coins.

## 5. UI map (Inferred)
- Minimal: role, crossings count, timer. Everything else is movement.

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Speed builds over time with a little slide on release, which gives a **skill-expressive movement feel**.
- Lateral dash.
- Clippable near-miss escapes.

## 7. Social
- Being caught turns you into a teammate of the chaser, which is a positive social flip.
- 30-player servers create crowd chaos.

## 8. Monetization
- Cosmetic knives plus a premium pass track. ×2 EXP is a mild accelerator.
- Need to verify whether the abilities are pay-to-win.

## 9. Live-ops
- Seasonal premium passes, and codes added around 2026-10-11.

## 10. Friction and risks
- Copycats with similar names exist already.
- The meta could become stale.
- The knife theme may affect the content maturity label.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable patterns:**
  - **zero-wait join into the live round**
  - an infection-style role flip so losing stays fun
  - momentum movement as a skill ceiling
  - pay on loss
  - a one-word promise
- **Protected:** the name, the knife theme, and the map.
- **Our alternative:** any round-based concept we make must drop a joining player straight into the active state, and must turn failure into a new role rather than a wait.
