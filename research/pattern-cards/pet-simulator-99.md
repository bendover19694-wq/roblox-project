# Pet Simulator 99 — pattern card
**Category:** idle / collection / trading (long-running)

**Observed (API, 2026-10-17):** 72,229 playing · 2.67B visits · server size 10 · created 2022-02-05 · 95% like · 13 passes (175–3,250 R)

**Evidence:** Observed description ("3,000+ pets") and passes. Loop details from prior knowledge (Inferred).

## 1. Audience, fantasy, promise
- Audience: collectors, traders, grinders, and players aged 9–14 who spend.
- Fantasy: commanding an army of pets that makes you rich.
- Promise: "Collect the coolest pets; they make you rich."

## 2. Player experience by time (Inferred)
- **15 s:** a pet follows you. Click breakables and coins burst out.
- **60 s:** buy the first egg, and a hatch animation reveals a pet.
- **5 min:** first zone gate is purchased with coins and new breakables appear.
- **30 min:** multiple zones, enchants and pet upgrades, a first glimpse of trading and leaderboards.

## 3. Loops
- **Core:** pets break objects → currency → eggs → stronger pets → next zone.
- **Meta:** zones, ranks, enchants, rare "Huge" pets.
- **Social:** trading plaza and clans.
- **Event:** frequent limited events and eggs.
- **Return tomorrow:** rank rewards, events, chasing rare drops.

## 4. Economy
- Several currencies, including a premium-like currency (Inferred).
- Rarity tiers with a famous chase tier.
- Sinks: eggs, zones, upgrades.
- Trading creates real-world-like value, which raises scam pressure.

## 5. UI map
- Dense HUD: currencies, pet count, quest pane, many side buttons.
- Frequent popups.
- The shop is one tap away.
- Desktop-first density is a known mobile pain point (Inferred).

## 6. Motion and feedback
- Coin bursts, number pops, and a hatch reveal with escalating rarity effects.
- Most of the emotional work is done by **the hatch reveal and rarity anticipation**.

## 7. Social
- Trading status, flexing Huge pets, clans.
- Safety risk: trade scams and value manipulation.

## 8. Monetization
- Passes are mostly **power and luck**: Lucky, Ultra Lucky, Huge Hunter, Super Drops, Auto Farm (Observed).
- Plus a developer-product premium currency and eggs (Inferred).
- Paywall perception is high.

## 9. Live-ops
- Frequent events and exclusive eggs, with a heavy creator/YouTube ecosystem (Inferred).

## 10. Friction and risks
- Pay-to-win perception, gambling-adjacent egg buying, and inflation.
- Long-tail CCU (about 72K) is far below the franchise's earlier peaks.

## 11. Transferable vs. protected
- **Transferable:**
  - the companion that does the work, so the player feels powerful
  - escalating rarity reveal
  - zone gates as visible next goals
- **Protected:** the pet-army sim identity, the "Huge" naming, art, UI.
- **Reject for us:**
  - paid luck multipliers
  - paid random eggs (requires a policy check, and conflicts with our ethics rule)
- **Our alternative:** companions earned through play or skill. Rarity is cosmetic or variety, not power. Reveals are earned, not bought.
